Privacy Policy – GS Ballistics

Last updated: September 10, 2026

Who we are

GS Ballistics is an Android app developed by Giorgio Saad. For any question about this policy, contact giorgio160586@email.com.

Data we collect

GS Ballistics does not collect data automatically and includes no advertising tracking or analytics. The only information that may ever be transmitted is:

  • Crash reports (optional): if the app closes unexpectedly, on the next launch you're asked whether you want to send an automatic technical report. Only if you confirm, the following is emailed to the developer: app version, device model, Android version, timestamp, and the technical error trace. No personally identifying data is included.
  • Contact form (optional): if you choose to contact the developer from the app's menu, you're taken to this site's contact form, where you may voluntarily enter your name, email, and message.

Data kept only on your device

App preferences (light/dark theme, language) are stored locally on your device only and are never transmitted anywhere.

Third-party services

Crash reports and contact messages are sent using EmailJS, which processes the data solely to deliver the email to the developer.

Data retention

Submitted reports are received as email by the developer and kept according to normal personal email practices.

Your rights

You may request deletion of any data submitted via a crash report or contact form at any time by writing to giorgio160586@email.com.

Changes to this policy

This page may be updated as the app's features change. The date at the top reflects the latest update.
